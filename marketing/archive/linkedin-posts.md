> CommuniHacks is a community hackathon happening on November 7-8 at Builders Club, where you build software to solve real problems faced by local nonprofits and community organizations.
> 
> Before the event, local nonprofits and community organizations submit problems they are facing, and CommuniHacks curates and accepts these on behalf of the hacker community. At the hackathon, every team is paired with one of these organizations and spends the weekend designing and building a software solution. If your solution is strong, the organization may invite your team to keep building with them after the event to fully integrate it into their operations. Top teams also compete for $5,000+ in prizes 🏆
