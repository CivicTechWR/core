## Description

> Got a community problem? Pitch it!
>
> Civic Tech's Season 9 starts soon. Anyone can share a community problem and find people who want to work on it, with you, over the next few months.
>
> **You do not need a solution.** You do not need a team, a plan, or a technical background. You need a problem you care about and three minutes. We do hope you can guide throughout the ~4 month season; but hey, let's cross that bridge when we get there!
>
> **How Pitch Night works**
> 🎤 3-minute pitch & 2 minutes of questions
> 💡 Focus on the *problem*, not the solution — who is affected, and what exists today
> 🤝 After the pitches, we will have an unstructured session where folks can chat with you about the problem (and possible solutions!)
>
> **Who pitches?** Beginners, students, public-sector folks, nonprofits, and curious neighbours. Everyone is welcome.
>
> **Not pitching?** You're our other half! Come find a project to join.
>

> 📍 The Builders Club (165 King Street W, Kitchener)
> 🍕 There shall be food.
